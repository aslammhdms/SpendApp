Dirham.ttf holds one character: U+20C3 UAE DIRHAM SIGN.

The outline comes from the "dirham" package, version 1.5.3
(https://github.com/pooyagolchian/dirham), MIT License, Copyright (c) Pooya
Golchian. That package takes the symbol from the Central Bank of the UAE.
Here the outline is scaled to the height of a capital letter, placed on the
baseline and given space on both sides, so it sits level with the digits
