Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

SpendSheet ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal expense tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Access

SpendSheet requests access to the following Google services through your Google account:

  • Google Sheets API — To create and manage a spreadsheet that stores your expense data (transactions, categories, accounts, budgets, and loans). SpendSheet asks only for access to files it creates itself (the drive.file permission); it cannot see your other spreadsheets or files.
  • Google Drive API — To find SpendSheet spreadsheets the app created earlier, so you can connect to one again.
  • Google Sign-In — To authenticate your identity and obtain your name, email address, and profile photo for display within the app.

If you turn on the optional Google Wallet payments feature (Android only), SpendSheet also uses:

  • Notification access — To read the payment notifications that the Google Wallet app shows after you pay, so the app can offer each payment as an expense for you to confirm. This feature is off by default. Android's notification access lets an app see notifications from every app; SpendSheet reads only those posted by Google Wallet and ignores all others without reading or storing them. You can turn the feature off in SpendSheet's settings, or withdraw notification access in your device settings, at any time.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device: Your financial data is stored locally in an SQLite database on your device. App preferences (currency, theme, notification settings) are stored in local shared preferences.
  • Google Wallet payments (optional): The title, text, and time of each captured Google Wallet notification are stored only on your device, and are read on your device to find the amount, merchant, and last four digits of the card. They are not synced to your spreadsheet. A payment reaches your spreadsheet only as an ordinary expense, after you confirm it. Turning the feature off deletes everything it has collected, including payments you have not reviewed yet. Right after a payment, SpendSheet can show its own notification with the merchant and amount as a reminder to add it; this is created on your device, hides the amount on a lock screen that hides sensitive content, and can be turned off in the app.
  • In your Google account: Your financial data is synced to a Google Sheets spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. This spreadsheet is created by and belongs to you. We do not have access to your Google Drive or spreadsheets.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your financial data on any external server.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal information to any third parties.
  • We do not access any Google data beyond what is described above.
  • We do not send notification content anywhere, and we do not read notifications from any app other than Google Wallet.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is never shared with third parties. All data remains either on your device or in your personal Google Drive account. The app communicates only with Google's APIs to sync your data to your own spreadsheet.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through your device settings.
  • Google Sheets data: Since the spreadsheet belongs to your Google account, you can view, edit, or delete it at any time through Google Sheets or Google Drive.
  • Revoking access: You can revoke SpendSheet's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions.

6. Security

SpendSheet uses Google's OAuth 2.0 authentication. The app does not store your Google password. Sign-in tokens are held by Google's sign-in service on your device; the app itself keeps only your account's name, email address and photo link, in encrypted storage. The app's data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer. All communication with Google's APIs is encrypted via HTTPS.

7. Children's Privacy

SpendSheet is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
